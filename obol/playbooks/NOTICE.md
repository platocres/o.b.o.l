# Playbooks — attribution

Playbooks are obol's own compositions: named, ordered sequences that reference
methodology-pack **action ids**. The ordering and the step selection here are
original obol work, not copied from any upstream source.

However, a step *invokes* a pack action, and some packs are derived from
GPL-3.0 sources (the Orange Cyberdefense AD mind map — see
[`../packs/NOTICE.md`](../packs/NOTICE.md)). When a playbook references such an
action, the action's own license and attribution continue to govern that
action's content. Playbooks do not fold pack content into themselves; they only
name actions by id, so the licensing boundary of the packs is preserved.
